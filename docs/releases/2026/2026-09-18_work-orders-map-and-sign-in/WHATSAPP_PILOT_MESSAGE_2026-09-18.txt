*iREPS MOBILE — NEW RELEASE*
*18 September 2026*

A new iREPS Mobile release is ready. Please install it before you start work.

*Install it over the app you have. Do NOT uninstall first* — uninstalling deletes any forms still waiting on your phone.

*WHAT'S NEW*

• *Search inside a batch*
In *My Work Orders*, open a batch and type a meter number, ERF number or street name in the search box.

• *Batch map*
Tap the map button on the batch. Every meter is a pin:
→ *S* — the Sales GPS point
→ *G* — the address point (not checked in the field)
→ *E* — the middle of the ERF
Tap a pin to get the same buttons as the list. The buttons at the top switch on *ERFs*, *Premises*, other *Sales* meters, *Me* (your position) and *Satellite*.

• *Finished meters are locked*
A meter that is already found shows as completed, below the open work, and its buttons are locked. You no longer waste a trip on it.

• *The app checks the batch first*
When you open a batch meter from anywhere — list, map, premise or ERF — you will see *"Checking the batch meter…"* for a moment. If the meter is already done, or the batch is no longer yours, the app tells you instead of letting you capture it for nothing.

• *My Work Orders comes back instantly*
The phone now loads only your own batches. Going back to the screen is instant. If your signal drops you will see *"Not up to date — reconnecting"*.

• *Sign in tells you what went wrong*
Instead of "Network error" every time, you now see one of:
→ *No connection* — check your signal
→ *Sign in failed* — email or password is not right
→ *Too many tries* — wait a few minutes
→ *Account stopped* — speak to your manager
It no longer spins forever.

• *Meter numbers: letters and digits only*
Spaces are removed and small letters become capitals by themselves. *Dashes, slashes, dots and other symbols are refused* — type the number without them.
Example: if the meter shows *1234-567*, type *1234567*.

*PLEASE TEST AND REPORT*

Please use the app normally and report anything that behaves differently — crashes, freezing, slow screens, GPS problems or failed submissions.

In particular:
• Open a batch, try the search and the map.
• Tell us how long *My Work Orders* takes to open the first time in the morning.
• Tell us if a meter number you need is refused.
